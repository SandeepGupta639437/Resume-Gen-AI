# 📌 Overview

ResumeGen AI is a full-stack AI-powered interview preparation platform.

Traditional resume screening tools mainly focus on ATS scores or keyword matching. ResumeGen AI goes a step further by analyzing:

- 📄 Candidate resume
- 💼 Target job/role
- 🧠 Technical skills
- 📊 Skill gaps
- 🎯 Role-specific requirements

and generates a structured interview preparation report.

The goal is simple:

> **Upload your resume → specify your target role → let AI generate a personalized interview preparation roadmap.**

---

# ✨ Features

## 📄 Resume Upload

Upload your resume as a PDF and let the backend extract the textual content automatically.

The application uses PDF parsing to convert the uploaded resume into text that can be processed by the AI pipeline.

---

## 🤖 AI-Powered Resume Analysis

The extracted resume information is analyzed using Google's Generative AI APIs.

The AI evaluates the candidate against the target role and generates structured insights.

---

## 🎯 Job Role Based Analysis

Users can specify the role they are preparing for.

For example:

Android Developer Intern
